Our Story

Founded in 2010 by Dr. Rajesh Sharma, Dentiva began with a simple mission: world-class dental treatment in a warm, anxiety-free environment. What started as a single-chair clinic in Bengaluru has grown into a full-service dental center trusted by over 10,000 families across India.

We believe dental care should be accessible, comfortable, and transparent. That's why we invest in the latest technology, continuous training, and a patient-first approach that puts your comfort at the center of everything we do.
